ABOUT

Communication through sign

SignAR turns supported Filipino Sign Language signs into text using your device’s camera. It is designed to support learning and everyday communication. The signs available depend on the recognition model in this version.

GET STARTED

How to use SignAR

  • Select Recognize and start the camera.
  • Keep your hands and upper body in view, then sign naturally.
  • Recognized signs appear in the message area. Copy or read the sentence aloud when you’re ready.
WHO IT SUPPORTS

Made for FSL learners

  • Filipino Sign Language signers and learners.
  • Families, interpreters, educators, and communication partners.
  • Organizations working to make sign-supported communication more accessible.
PRIVACY
